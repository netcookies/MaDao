# MaDao Trademark Policy

This policy protects the MaDao project identity while allowing normal community contribution, discussion, and compatibility work.

## Protected Marks

- The name `MaDao` and the Chinese name `码到`
- Project logos, icons, badges, and visual identifiers
- Official release names, domains, repositories, package names, and distribution channels controlled by the project maintainers

## Allowed Use

You may use the protected marks without prior written permission when you:

- Refer to the official MaDao project truthfully.
- Link to the official repository, documentation, or releases.
- Submit issues, pull requests, reviews, documentation, translations, or other contributions to the official project.
- Describe compatibility with MaDao, for example `compatible with MaDao`, as long as the statement is accurate and not misleading.
- Make unmodified copies of official releases under the project license, provided that copyright, license, and trademark notices remain intact.

## Modified Versions

You may create, use, and publish modified versions under the terms of the project license, but modified versions must:

- Use a clearly different name and branding.
- Clearly state that they are modified and are not official MaDao releases.
- Avoid names, logos, package identifiers, domains, screenshots, or release channels that are likely to confuse users about origin, sponsorship, or endorsement.
- Remove or replace official MaDao logos, app icons, and release branding unless you have written permission.

## Not Allowed

Without prior written permission from the project maintainers, you may not:

- Present a modified version as `MaDao`, an official MaDao release, or an endorsed MaDao distribution.
- Use the protected marks in a product, service, company name, domain, package, app store listing, or hosted service in a way that suggests official status.
- Use confusingly similar names or visual branding for a fork, derivative work, or commercial offering.
- Register trademarks, domains, social accounts, package names, or app listings that contain or imitate the protected marks.
- Remove required attribution from official project materials.

## Contributions

By submitting a contribution to this repository, you agree that your contribution may be distributed as part of MaDao under the repository license. Contributions do not grant you ownership of the protected marks.

## Permission

For trademark permission, contact the project maintainers through the official repository. Permission may be limited, revoked, or conditioned on additional attribution, quality, or branding requirements.

This trademark policy is separate from the software license. The license grants rights to copy, modify, and distribute the software; this policy governs use of the project identity and protected marks.
